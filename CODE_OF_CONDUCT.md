# Code of conduct

This applies across all ChurchBridge repositories.

## The expectation

Be decent to people. Assume the person on the other end is acting in good faith,
is dealing with constraints you can't see, and is worth the courtesy of a
straight answer.

Concretely, that means:

- **Criticize work, not people.** "This drops frames under load" is useful.
  "This is amateur" is not.
- **Disagree with reasons.** Technical disagreement is welcome and often the
  most valuable thing in a thread. Bring the argument.
- **Be patient with beginners.** Someone setting up their first church A/V
  system is not obliged to know what an ERB filterbank is.
- **Respect the context.** This project serves congregations, and people from
  many traditions and none use and discuss it. Keep discussion on the software
  and the problem it solves.

## Not acceptable

Harassment, personal attacks, demeaning comments about anyone's background,
beliefs, or identity, sustained disruption, or publishing someone's private
information.

The same standard applies whether you are contributing code, filing an issue,
or answering a question.

## Reporting

Report a problem to the maintainer through [dhaines.dev](https://dhaines.dev),
or by email if you already have an address. Reports are handled privately.

Include what happened, where, and links if you have them. You will get a
response.

## Enforcement

The maintainer will respond in proportion to the situation — a clarifying reply,
a request to change tone, editing or removing content, or blocking an account
for serious or repeated problems.

This project has one maintainer, so enforcement is one person's judgment. If you
think that judgment was wrong, say so; that is not itself a violation.
